# Lumière demo Q&A

- **Did money move?** No. Authorization, capture and void are simulated locally. The merchant order and stock change are persisted by the local Lumière storefront. A `pi_mock_…` reference is not a Stripe PaymentIntent.
- **What actually ran?** A declared browser agent visited both prepared storefronts. An MCP client then discovered the exact SKU, requested a quote, completed the simulated purchase and retrieved the order. The open checkout page updated from the same service; the merchant record matched it.
- **Is this an autonomous shopping assistant?** The stage runner is a deterministic MCP client with a fixed purchase request. It demonstrates the interface an assistant can use and the evidence returned by the purchase flow.
- **Did you automatically fix a website?** This demonstration uses prepared before/after versions of a fictional store. It proves their different behavior and the connected purchase. The scan/indexing workstream is separate.
- **Why the wrong size and blocked checkout?** They are deliberate before-store failures. Selecting 75 ml adds 150 ml, and the store blocks declared agents at checkout. The after-store preserves the chosen SKU and permits the agent.
- **How do you prevent duplicate orders?** Confirmation carries an idempotency key. The checkout persists its payment and merchant references and reconciles a pending merchant order before placing another. The rehearsal repeats confirmation and checks one order, one authorization and one capture.
- **What if the price or stock changes?** The connector reads current merchant price and stock and checks the cart total before placement. A changed price requires fresh confirmation. Unavailable stock prevents purchase. Standard shipping is free from $65; express is $14.95.
- **What if capture fails?** The service cancels the merchant order, restores stock and releases authorization. Lost order and cancellation responses are covered by restart/retry integration tests.
- **What is standardized?** The after-store publishes a Shopify-shaped product feed, structured product data, shopping instructions and a UCP profile. ShopperZero exposes MCP checkout tools and REST sessions. We have not established full UCP conformance or certification.
- **Where is the proof?** The individual checkout timeline, the `LDP…` merchant receipt and the saved rehearsal evidence all identify the same purchase. The evidence also records the repeated confirmation and payment-operation counts.
